Android PWA Background Execution Notes

All timers use wall-clock timestamps, so elapsed time and midnight rollovers remain 100% accurate across screen lock, app switching, or browser closure. However, Android Chrome suspends background tabs and Service Workers when the screen is locked, so loud audio playback cannot be guaranteed while the OS sleeps; the app immediately reconciles missed completions and alerts on wake.
